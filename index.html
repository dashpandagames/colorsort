How to play

  1. 1

    Pick up a bottle

    Tap any bottle with liquid in it. It lifts up.

  2. 2

    Pour it

    Tap another bottle. You can pour if it's empty or has the same colour on top, and it has room.

  3. 3

    Sort every colour

    Finish when each bottle holds a single colour. Fewer moves = more stars ⭐

Questions

Is Color Sort free?

Yes. The whole game is free. It's supported by ads, and there's an optional one-time purchase to remove them, plus optional cosmetic themes. You never have to pay to finish a level.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed and the game works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection when you use them.

I'm stuck on a level!

Use Undo or Restart (always free), or tap Hint to see a good next pour. An empty bottle is your most valuable tool, so try not to fill it too early.

Will my purchases carry over to a new phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore purchases in Settings or the Shop.

Email dashpandagames@gmail.com. We read every message.
