Privacy Policy

App: Color Sort (Android package com.dashpandagames.colorsort) and this website
Developer: Dash Panda Games · Contact: dashpandagames@gmail.com
Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information Color Sort ("the app") and this website handle, and why. We built the app to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and we don't run our own servers that receive your data.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device: completed levels, stars, hints, settings, owned cosmetics and the puzzle you're currently playing. This data stays on your device. We don't receive it. It's deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data. The browser version on this website saves the same kind of progress in your browser's local storage, and it never leaves your browser.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, and app-set ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent or choices through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change them any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Ads are only shown when your device is online.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Remove Ads and cosmetic themes) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Analytics and crash reports

The current version of the app does not send analytics or crash reports to us or to any analytics provider. Basic error information is kept only on your device. If we add an analytics or crash-reporting service in a future update, we will update this policy first and respect your consent choices. You will also be able to switch it off in Settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads and process purchases
  • Vibrate: optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above

6. Children

Color Sort is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, ads or analytics of its own. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Data sharing, sale and retention

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share it, other than through the Google services described above. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies. Data on your device is kept until you delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through your device settings and Google's tools (for example My Ad Center). You're also welcome to email us and we'll help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. We'll change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
